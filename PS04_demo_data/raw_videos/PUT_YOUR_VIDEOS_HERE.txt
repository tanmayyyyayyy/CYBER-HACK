Place your raw MP4 / MOV / AVI / MKV video files here.

Supported extensions: .mp4 .mov .avi .mkv .3gp .m4v .wmv .mpg .mpeg .flv .webm

After placing your videos run:
  python3 PS04_demo_data/_tools/build_ps04.py init PS04_demo_data/

This will scan this folder and generate a clip_manifest.csv template.
Fill in the blank columns (person_id, role, timestamp offsets, etc.)
then run:
  python3 PS04_demo_data/_tools/build_ps04.py build PS04_demo_data/

See PS04_demo_data/HOW_TO_USE_YOUR_VIDEOS.md for full instructions.
